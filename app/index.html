    Roadmap to platinum

    Seven stages from the first mission to the platinum, built from the official trophy descriptions. Work the stages in order; the Next-up planner always points at your next unearned trophy.

    Missables & time-sensitive trophies

    One confirmed missable risk, one time-sensitive strand, and the honest news: everything else stays available after the credits.

    Everything else stays open

    All six side-story finales and the whole 100% checklist stay available after the credits, including the 35-task 100% checklist itself (it only appears after the story, under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion — but work done earlier still counts). Cheats switch off trophies and saving, so keep them off on your trophy save.

    Save discipline

    Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter plus one untouched save from just before the final mission. There are 15 manual slots plus 1 autosave. There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus at launch, so a missed Chapter 1 spot can force a second playthrough.

    About this guide

    Grand Theft Auto VI released 19 November 2026. Rockstar, Sony and Microsoft have published no separate official trophy-list page — the "official list" here is the PlayStation 5 trophy list as mirrored by Exophase and PSNProfiles, which agree on every name, description and grade. Trophy names and descriptions are quoted exactly as PlayStation publishes them; every guide is written in our own words from those descriptions and the cited sources, and says plainly where a method is still unconfirmed at launch. Press estimates put a full platinum run near 200 hours; that is a press estimate, not an official figure. No Rockstar logo or official artwork is used anywhere in this guide.
